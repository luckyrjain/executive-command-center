# Scheduling

Meetings and what's needed to prepare for them.

## Language

**Meeting**:
A first-class record of a meeting — agenda, preparation notes, a notes summary — with its own status
lifecycle, independent of any calendar entry. May optionally link to one
[CalendarEvent](../calendar/CONTEXT.md); when linked, its timing is derived read-only from that event. When
standalone, it owns its own timing directly.

**MeetingPack**:
A deterministic, frozen snapshot built for one Meeting: participants, timeline, commitments, decisions, notes,
risks, dependencies, and evidence gaps — plus optional AI-generated enrichment. Owned and generated by
[Attention](../attention/CONTEXT.md), not by Scheduling, despite being entirely about Meetings.
_Avoid_: Brief (the term used for this concept in `docs/domain/DOMAIN-MODEL.md`, but no class or table named
Brief exists in code — MeetingPack is the real name)
